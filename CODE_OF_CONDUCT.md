# Code of Conduct

Punctim is built by people who disagree about protocols, codecs and priorities. That is
expected. This document is about how we disagree.

## What we expect

- **Argue about the work, not the person.** Criticise code, specs and claims as hard as they
  deserve; leave people's character, background and competence out of it.
- **Be direct and be kind.** Say what is wrong and why. Assume the other person is acting in
  good faith until they show otherwise.
- **Back claims with evidence.** In this project a claim is worth what its test, vector or
  measurement is worth. Asking "how do you know?" is normal and is not an insult.
- **Make room for newcomers.** Answer the question that was asked, point to the document that
  answers it, and remember that everyone started somewhere.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private messages.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, body, ability or origin.
- Sexual language or imagery directed at people, and unwanted attention or contact.
- Threats, intimidation, and encouraging others to do any of these.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions after being asked to stop.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project is present: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat
channels and events run for the project, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to DeMoD LLC at **alh477@demod.ltd** (the same address as
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)). Say what happened, where, and who was involved; links or
screenshots help. Reports are kept as confidential as the situation allows, and nobody will
be penalised for reporting in good faith.

## What happens next

The maintainers will look at every report and decide what to do. Depending on what happened,
that may be a private word, a public correction, removal of comments or contributions, or a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces. The response will match the harm, and the
maintainers will tell the reporter what was done where that is possible.
